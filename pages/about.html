Our Story

Meet Brenda O. Koger

Brenda O. Koger founded Pink Petals Facility Solutions with a clear vision: to build a cleaning company that clients could trust completely — one that combined the reliability of a national brand with the personal care of a local partner. Based in Northwest Indiana, Brenda leads every aspect of the company with a standard she simply calls "inspection-ready" — not just clean, but provably, verifiably clean. Under her leadership, Pink Petals has grown from a local residential service into a registered government contractor serving corporate, healthcare, education, and municipal clients throughout the region.
