Our Green Commitment

Clean Spaces Shouldn't Cost the Earth

At Pink Petals, eco-friendly isn't a marketing term — it's a core operating principle. Every product we use, every method we employ, is chosen with health and sustainability in mind.

Why It Matters

The Problem with Conventional Cleaning

Traditional commercial cleaning products often contain harsh chemicals — ammonia, chlorine bleach, synthetic fragrances, and VOCs — that can cause respiratory irritation, skin reactions, and long-term health problems for building occupants and cleaning staff.

Indoor Air Pollution

Conventional cleaners release VOCs that pollute indoor air — often worse than outdoor air pollution in commercial buildings.

Health Risks to Occupants

Chemical residues on surfaces and in the air contribute to headaches, asthma, allergies, and eye/skin irritation.

Environmental Damage

Harsh chemicals wash into waterways and contribute to ecosystem damage and pollution when products are disposed of.

The Pink Petals Solution

We replace harmful conventional products with safer, equally effective eco-friendly alternatives at every step of our cleaning process.

Our Approach

How We Clean Differently

Our green cleaning program is built on three pillars: safer products, smarter methods, and sustainable practices.

EPA-Registered Products

All disinfectants and cleaning agents are EPA-registered and on the List N for safety and efficacy against pathogens.

Green Seal Compatible

Our product choices align with Green Seal GS-42 standards, supporting LEED certification for your building.

Microfiber Technology

Microfiber cloths and mops remove up to 99% of bacteria using less water and fewer chemicals — cleaner and more efficient.

Reduced Waste

Concentrated formulas, reusable supplies, and responsible disposal practices minimize packaging waste and environmental impact.

Safe for Sensitive Groups

Our products are safe for children, elderly individuals, allergy sufferers, and people with chemical sensitivities.

Continuous Improvement

We stay current with new sustainable technologies and regularly evaluate and upgrade our product and method selections.
