What We Deliver

Core Service Capabilities

Professional facility services designed to meet government performance standards — scalable to any scope, agency, or facility type.

Custodial & Janitorial Services

Daily, weekly, and monthly janitorial programs tailored to government facilities, agency offices, and multi-tenant buildings.

Day Porter Services

On-site day porter personnel maintaining cleanliness, restocking supplies, and ensuring facility readiness throughout operating hours.

Emergency Cleaning Response

Rapid-response cleaning and remediation for unplanned events, post-incident cleanup, and urgent facility restoration needs.

High-Touch Surface Disinfection

Targeted disinfection of high-contact surfaces, restrooms, and common areas to meet agency infection-control standards.

Post-Construction Cleanup

Thorough construction and renovation cleanup — debris removal, deep cleaning, and pre-occupancy inspection-ready preparation.

Facility Support Services

Comprehensive facility operations support including floor care, carpet extraction, window cleaning, and light maintenance assistance.
