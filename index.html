What We Do

Core Capabilities

01 NAICS 561720

Janitorial Services

Comprehensive daily, weekly, and custom-scheduled cleaning programs for federal buildings, commercial offices, and institutional facilities. OSHA-compliant, inspection-ready results.

02 NAICS 561210

Facility Management

Full-scope operations management coordinating maintenance, cleaning, vendor oversight, and compliance reporting for multi-site portfolio clients and government agencies.

03 NAICS 561790

Specialty Cleaning

Floor care, carpet extraction, high-dusting, post-construction cleanup, pressure washing, and facility restoration for demanding environments and critical deadlines.

04 NAICS 561740

Window & Glass Cleaning

Interior and exterior window cleaning for commercial buildings, federal facilities, and institutional campuses. Streak-free, professionally executed at any height.

05 NAICS 562910

Waste & Remediation

Hazardous and non-hazardous waste removal, recycling program management, and environmental compliance services for regulated industries and government sites.

06 PSC S201

Disinfection & Sanitation

CDC and EPA-compliant disinfection protocols. Electrostatic spraying, touchpoint sanitization, and infection control programs for healthcare and high-traffic facilities.
