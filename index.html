About Pink Petals Inc

Excellence You Can See.
Service You Can Trust.

Pink Petals Inc is a women-owned and minority-owned commercial janitorial and facility services company founded by Brenda O. Koger in Merrillville, Indiana.

We proudly serve government agencies, healthcare facilities, educational institutions, commercial offices, industrial facilities, and property management companies throughout Northwest Indiana, Illinois, and surrounding areas.

Our mission is simple: create clean, safe, and healthy environments for every client — delivering premium results with environmentally responsible practices and unwavering attention to detail.

Commercial Janitorial Services

Daily, weekly, or custom cleaning for offices and commercial facilities.

Floor Care

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning
