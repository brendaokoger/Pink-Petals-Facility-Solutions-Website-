Our Approach

The Pink Petals Clean Air System

A three-step methodology built around healthier indoor environments — not just clean surfaces.

1

Assess

We evaluate your facility's specific needs, traffic patterns, surface types, and indoor air quality concerns before recommending any service program.

2

Clean

We apply our eco-friendly, non-toxic protocol — targeting high-touch surfaces, eliminating pathogens, and protecting air quality with every product we use.

3

Maintain

Ongoing scheduled service keeps your space consistently clean. We document every visit and remain accountable to the standards we set at the start.
