Our Story

Built on Standards.
Driven by Purpose.

Pink Petals Facility Solutions was founded by Brenda O. Koger with a singular conviction: every facility—whether a federal courthouse, a hospital, or a corporate campus—deserves services that meet the highest standards of cleanliness, safety, and professionalism.

As a Women-Owned and Minority-Owned small business headquartered in Merrillville, Indiana, we bring enterprise-level precision to every contract—from single-facility accounts to multi-site government agreements.
